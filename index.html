TikTok Clip

A personal tool that turns my Twitch stream highlights into vertical TikTok videos.

How it works

  1. While I'm live on Twitch, I mark moments worth clipping.
  2. After the stream, TikTok Clip cuts each marked moment into a short vertical video with captions.
  3. I review every clip and approve the ones I like.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to my own TikTok inbox as drafts. I publish them myself from the TikTok app.

What it does with TikTok

  • Logs in to my own TikTok account only.
  • Uploads my own clips as drafts. It never publishes automatically.
  • Doesn't access other users' accounts or data.

TikTok Clip is used only by its owner and isn't offered to the public.
