Privacy Policy

TikTok Clip · Last updated: October 21, 2026

TikTok Clip is a personal tool run by Simon to turn their own Twitch stream highlights into TikTok drafts. It is used only by its owner on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile info (user.info.basic), permission to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload), and, if enabled, the owner's own video list and view/like counts (video.list).
  • Twitch: the owner's own past broadcasts and stream markers.

How the data is used

Data is used only to create video clips from the owner's streams, upload them as drafts to the owner's TikTok account, and track how those clips perform, so future clips can be edited better.

Storage and sharing

  • Login tokens, clips and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Stream transcripts are sent to an AI service chosen by the owner to suggest clip edits.
  • No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to track anyone.
  • The app does not collect data about other TikTok users.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.
